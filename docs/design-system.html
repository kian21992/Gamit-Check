✓

Accessibility check

✓All text/background pairs meet at least 4.5:1 contrast.
✓Semantic header, nav, main, footer and button elements.
✓Meaningful images have alt text; decorative art is hidden.
✓Every form control has a matching visible label.
✓Keyboard focus is visible and every action is reachable by Tab.
